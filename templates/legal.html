Medical Disclaimer & Emergency Advisory

MindSphere is NOT a clinical diagnostic tool or emergency service.

The risk calculations, SHAP explainability visualizations, and lifestyle tips generated by this application are provided strictly for informational and educational self-reflection. They are derived from machine learning models trained on statistical patterns and do not constitute medical advice, psychiatric diagnosis, treatment planning, or clinical prognostication.

Always seek the counsel of a licensed physician, psychiatrist, clinical psychologist, or other qualified healthcare provider regarding any questions or concerns you have about your mental or emotional health. Never delay seeking professional care or disregard medical guidance based on insights generated on this platform.

In Crisis or Need Immediate Support?

If you or someone you know is in acute distress or experiencing thoughts of self-harm, please contact your local emergency services (e.g. 911 in the US/Canada, 112 in Europe/India, 999 in the UK) or reach out to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline immediately.

Privacy Policy

Your trust and data privacy are our highest priorities. This section outlines how we collect, process, and protect your information:

  • Data Collection: We collect discrete self-reported factors (such as age range, occupational sector, indoor duration, and subjective stress levels) solely to feed our ML models and compute your risk score.
  • Data Storage: Assessment records are saved securely in our database linked to your user profile so you can inspect historical trends. We never license, sell, or rent your data to data brokers or third-party advertisers.
  • De-identification & Anonymity: While user credentials (email/username) are maintained for authentication, the feature vector processed by the predictive algorithms is anonymized during inference.
  • Account Rights: You may request full deletion of your assessment history and account at any time by reaching out to our support channel.
